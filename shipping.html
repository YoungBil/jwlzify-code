We partner with the world's most trusted white-glove courier services to ensure your piece arrives safely and discreetly.

verified

FedEx International Priority

Primary carrier for North America and Europe. Full signature-on-delivery and real-time tracking included on all shipments.

verified

DHL Express

Used for Middle East, Asia-Pacific, and select international destinations. Proactive customs clearance support included.

verified

Brinks Secure Logistics

Available on request for high-value pieces exceeding $25,000. Insured door-to-door with armed courier service.

All shipments are fully insured for the declared value of your piece. Tracking details are emailed automatically at dispatch.

We ship to over 80 countries worldwide. International orders ship from our atelier in Antwerp, Belgium.

Duties & Taxes

Import duties and local taxes are the responsibility of the recipient and vary by destination country. We declare the full insured value on all customs forms.

Restricted Countries

We currently cannot ship to sanctioned destinations. If your country is not available at checkout, please contact our concierge team for alternative arrangements.

Currency

All prices are displayed and charged in USD. Your card issuer applies the conversion rate at time of purchase.

Insurance

Every international shipment is insured for full replacement value. In the event of loss or damage in transit, we will remake your piece at no additional cost.

support_agent

For shipping questions specific to your country, reach our concierge team at hello@jwlzify.com

The unboxing is part of the experience. Every JWLZIFY piece ships in our signature presentation packaging, designed to reflect the craftsmanship inside.

1

Signature Lacquered Box

Your piece arrives in a rigid matte-black lacquered box with gold JWLZIFY embossing. Lined with custom-cut velvet to cradle your piece securely.

2

Authentication Card & Certificate

A hand-numbered certificate of authenticity, GIA stone documentation (where applicable), and your personalized design card are included with every order.

3

Discreet Outer Shipping

The presentation box is secured inside a plain outer carton with no external branding. Only a shipping label and barcode are visible externally.

4

Sustainable Materials

All packaging materials are FSC-certified and fully recyclable. Our velvet inserts are made from recycled textile fibres. We use zero single-use plastics.
